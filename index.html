Interactive SOP Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

What makes this different from static documentation?

Static SOPs get forgotten in PDFs. This engine renders actionable, interactive checklists with embedded task timers that persist completion state in your browser, keeping operators focused on time-boxed execution.

Where is the checklist progress saved?

Every checkbox toggle and operator note is immediately serialized into localStorage. You can refresh your browser or close the tab, and your runbook state will remain intact.

How do I paste the runbook into Notion?

Click "Export to Notion". The tool copies formatted GitHub-flavored markdown with Notion-compatible callout headers, to-do checkboxes (- [ ]), and bullet items. Simply press Ctrl+V inside any Notion page.

Is this tool private?

100% private. All SOP data, checklist states, operator notes, and API keys are stored exclusively in your browser's local memory with zero remote logging.
